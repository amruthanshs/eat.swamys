How to order

If you've ordered tenders and fries before, you already know how this works.

  1. Pick your protein

    Chicken tenders or popcorn paneer.

  2. Pick your heat

    Five levels, so everyone at the table gets their own.

  3. Pick your sauces

    On the side, so you can dip and compare.

  4. Make it a Swamy's

    Add a masala chai with a Parle-G biscuit.

Our story

Our story

It started on a road trip, looking for a taste of home.

"I was born in India, and when my family first moved to the US, we loved to take road trips to explore our new home. While driving we were always on the lookout for two things: a taste of home, and vegetarian options. Neither was readily available."

Swamy's is the place I wished we'd found. It's the food I grew up on, served the way I always wished I could order it: fast, easy and full of flavor. Crispy tenders, golden fries, sauces I'd put on everything, and a cup of chai to bring it home.

Real Indian flavors, no watering down. Come hungry. Eat with your hands. Stay a while.

AmruthFounder, Swamy's
